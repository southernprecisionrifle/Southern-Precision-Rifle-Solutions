About

Southern Precision Rifle Solutions focuses on dependable, well-balanced precision rifle builds, quality components, and a straightforward customer experience. The company is owned by Southern Auction Solutions, Inc., the FFL holder for the business.

Built Around the Customer

Every project starts with a conversation. We look at intended use, preferred platform, budget, ergonomics, and overall goals before making recommendations.

Licensed & Straightforward

Southern Auction Solutions, Inc. is the FFL holder and handles regulated transfers for the business. We also handle NFA transfers and electronic Form 4 filings for suppressors in accordance with applicable law.

The Process

A simple four-step path keeps expectations clear from the first conversation to the completed project.

STEP 01

Consult

We talk through your goals, intended use, preferences, and budget.

STEP 02

Plan

We outline the direction of the build and confirm the major component choices.

STEP 03

Build

The project comes together with attention to fit, finish, compatibility, and presentation.

STEP 04

Complete

We review the finished project and make sure the customer understands the completed setup.

All services and transfers are handled in accordance with applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations.
